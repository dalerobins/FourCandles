# The Short Sighted Optician — bench measurements

- Characterise donor strings: polarity, wire topology, voltage/current sweep, branch ballast, conductor resistance and voltage drop.
- Scope LED current across a sense resistor and optical output with photodiode and suitable transimpedance amplifier. Avoid saturation, ambient light and aliasing; use sufficient bandwidth and sample rate.
- Measure ripple and modulation at full brightness and several dim settings, especially lowest useful glow. Record frequency, depth and waveform. Separate intentional occasional events from unwanted periodic modulation.
- Compare static and active LED resting brightness and colour. Measure supply and gate behaviour through reset and brownout.
- Test full shunt current, resistor and FET temperatures, simultaneous events, long string voltage drop and power injection.
- Record random interval and duration histograms, check repeated power cycles for synchronisation.
- Test visual effect in a dark room; tune rarity and depth. No absolute flicker-free claims until measured.

Record supply voltage, current, scope settings, component revisions and raw captures alongside each result. Target: low light, no unwanted visible temporal modulation, rare natural events.
